The row of rings at the top of a feed: one per person, bright while they have something new, muted once the viewer has seen it all. Pressing a ring opens the stories at the first one not yet seen, and the next visit, even after a reload, remembers where the viewer got to.

## Try it [#try-it]

Every version imports `./api`, which stands in for your backend: `groups` is the people a viewer follows with the stories each posted, in the shape of the player's `StoriesGroup`. Replace it with your own feed.

## Why it works [#why-it-works]

- **One controller remembers for both.** `createStoriesViewedStateController` is created once and goes to the ring list and the player as `viewed`. The rings read from it, the player records every story it shows into it, and it keeps that in `localStorage` under `storageKey`, so a reload changes nothing. See remembering what was seen for [React](/docs/stories-player#remembering-what-was-seen), [Vue](/docs/vue-stories-player#remembering-what-was-seen), and [Angular](/docs/angular-stories-player#remembering-what-was-seen).
- **The player picks up where the viewer left off.** Given `viewed`, the player opens a person on their first story not yet seen, or from the start once everything was seen. The recipe only passes the person, never a story index; the controller's `resumeStoryIndex` answers that, see [its reference](/docs/stories-core#storiesviewedstatecontroller).
- **A ring shows two states.** Bright while there is something left to watch, muted once a person was watched to the end. Progress inside a person is the player's progress bar, not the ring. See [StoriesRing](/docs/stories-player#storiesring).
- **The ring colours follow the page.** The two states are the tokens `--rk-stories-ring-active-gradient` and `--rk-stories-ring-viewed-color`, and the watched one defaults to a faint white made for a dark page. On a light page set it on `.rk-stories-ring-list`, as this demo does in the light theme, not on `:root`: the player's desktop carousel draws rings with the same tokens on its dark backdrop. The tokens are in the [theming table](/docs/stories-player#theming).
- **No extra wiring for the controller.** The player is always rendered and opened with `isOpen`, so it reads the stored state itself before it chooses the first story. Mount it only while it is open and the page has to attach the controller first, as [the controller reference](/docs/stories-core#storiesviewedstatecontroller) explains.
- **Focus comes back to the right ring.** As it closes, the player returns focus to the ring that opened it, as its [accessibility](/docs/stories-player#accessibility) section describes. That happens after the close handler has run, so the recipe waits one task, then scrolls the ring of the last person watched into view and focuses it.
- **Where else it can be kept.** `storage` takes your own adapter, a server for instance, so what was seen follows the viewer across devices. `ttlMs` lets a person's entry expire, and `maxTracks` caps how many people are kept. They are in the [controller config](/docs/stories-core#config-storiesviewedstatecontrollerconfig).
