A feed that never ends has to fetch while the viewer watches: early enough that the next post is already there, with something to show while a page is on its way or has failed, and without a request left running after the feed is gone.

## Try it [#try-it]

Every version imports `./api`, which stands in for your backend: `fetchPosts(page, signal)` returns one page of posts and stops when the signal aborts. Replace it with a call to your API, and drop `failNextRequest`, which only drives the demo's failure switch.

## Why it works [#why-it-works]

- **The count grows, the slides do not.** `count` is the number of posts plus one, and only three slides are ever in the DOM, so a feed of thousands costs the same as a feed of ten. See [virtualization](/docs/core/guide#virtualization).
- **The last slide is the status.** The extra slide past the posts shows "Loading…" while a page is on its way and the error with Retry when it failed, so the viewer never swipes into nothing. It is one more case in the [itemBuilder](/docs/react/guide#itembuilder-pattern), which renders whatever the index stands for.
- **Fetch ahead, from the slide change.** `afterChange` asks for the next page once the viewer is three posts from the end. A failed page waits for Retry, so swiping back and forth does not repeat a failing request. The callback is listed with the other events for [React](/docs/react/api#callbacks), [Vue](/docs/vue/api#events), and [Angular](/docs/angular/api#outputs).
- **Keys follow the posts.** `keyExtractor` returns each post's id, so a slide keeps its element when a page lands behind it instead of being rebuilt from its index. It is a prop of [React](/docs/react/api#reel-props), [Vue](/docs/vue/api#props), and [Angular](/docs/angular/api#inputs) alike.
- **Images load before their slide.** A slide further down is not mounted yet, because only the visible range is, as [virtualization](/docs/core/guide#virtualization) explains. So each new page's images are requested as soon as the page arrives and come from the browser cache when their slide mounts.
- **Keys and wheel are off on purpose.** `enableNavKeys` listens on the whole window and `enableWheel` holds the page's scroll while the pointer is over the feed. Both suit a feed that fills the screen and fight a page that holds one, as this one does, so here they stay off and two buttons call `prev()` and `next()` on the [Reel API](/docs/react/api#reelapi-methods) instead. Swiping works either way.
- **Nothing outlives the feed.** Each request carries an [`AbortController`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/AbortController), aborted when the feed unmounts, so a late response never writes into a feed that is gone.
